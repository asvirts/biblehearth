# BibleHearth

BibleHearth helps readers read Scripture and study its meaning across Bible translations.

## Language

**Verse hub**:
A page comparing one verse across a curated set of licensed Bible translations.
_Avoid_: Translation-specific verse page

**Rendering**:
The text of a verse in a particular Bible translation. A rendering that the translation does not contain is distinct from one that is temporarily unavailable.
_Avoid_: Missing verse, when retrieval failed
