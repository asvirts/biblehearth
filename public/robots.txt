# Search engines and AI answer engines: full access, at a measured pace.
#
# Crawl-delay is here because an uncached page is not just a render — it is a
# licensed Scripture fetch against a rate-limited app key. Googlebot ignores the
# directive and paces itself from Search Console; Ahrefs Site Audit and most
# others honour it. Bingbot honours it too, which is why it has its own group
# below rather than being paced from here.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /read
Disallow: /api/
Crawl-delay: 5

# Bingbot, paced faster than the long tail.
#
# Bing's guidelines treat crawl capacity as something it allocates from site
# health and crawl value, and ask that pacing be shaped in Bing Webmaster Tools
# (Crawl Control, which is hour-by-hour) rather than flattened by robots.txt.
# Five seconds is a ceiling of ~17k URLs a day applied to a crawler we actively
# want; one second is still bounded — well under what the app key can absorb —
# and lets a newly briefed chapter be picked up the day it ships.
#
# The two Disallow lines are repeated on purpose, not by accident. Robots.txt
# group matching is winner-takes-all: the moment a crawler finds a group naming
# it, the `*` group stops applying entirely. Without these lines here, giving
# Bingbot its own group would have handed it /read and /api/.
User-agent: bingbot
Allow: /
Disallow: /read
Disallow: /api/
Crawl-delay: 1
